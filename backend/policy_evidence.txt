INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY

Document version: 2.1
Approved by: Chief Information Security Officer
Approval date: 2025-01-15
Review cycle: Annual

1. PURPOSE
This policy defines the information security objectives of the organization
and affirms the strategic importance of protecting information assets in line
with our corporate goals.

2. SCOPE
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and external partners.

3. REVIEW AND COMMUNICATION
This policy is reviewed annually and communicated to all employees via the
company intranet. Non-compliance may result in disciplinary action.

4. LEGAL AND CONTRACTUAL REQUIREMENTS
The policy integrates applicable legal, regulatory, and contractual
requirements relevant to information security.
